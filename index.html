Questions, answered.

Where is Las Palmas Auto Repair?

4822 Compton Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90011 — on Compton Avenue in South LA. Tap "Get Directions" above for a map.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM–3:00 PM, closed Sundays.

Do you do smog tests?

Yes — there is a Smog Test Center on site. Customers have had a repair done and passed smog the same day.

Do you work on all makes and models?

Yes — everyday cars, light trucks, and fleet vehicles of all makes and models.

Do I need an appointment?

Calling ahead at (323) 234-6855 is the best way to secure a slot — describe the problem and the team will advise. Walk-ins are taken when the workload allows.

Who will work on my car?

Jose is the mechanic customers name in their reviews — "Jose and his team" handle the work, and customers praise how thoroughly they answer questions.
